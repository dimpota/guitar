                                 IF NOT NOW

Use a capo barred at the second fret to match the pitch of this one. Follow
the transcription as you would any TAB, disregarding the fact that you're
two frets up. A pitch of "2" is played by plucking the notated string two
frets above the capo, not the nut.

          Gmaj7                                    D

This is repeated a few times; when Tracy sings "then- why make your
promises" the chords change between D, Bm, A, Fm, and G. Again remember that
the song is capoed at the second fret, so the shape of the D will be a C,
the Bm and Am, etc. In case it's still unclear, here are the actual chord
forms to shape:
D (x32010), Bm (x02210), A (320003), F#m (022000, and G (133211).

Change to an F#m7 (010000) when the sequence is repeated immediately
thereafter. Change between G and A on the bridge to the repeat.
